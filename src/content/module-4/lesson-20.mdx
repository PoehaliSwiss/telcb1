## 📅 Part 1: The 24-Hour Protocol

**Rule #1: The "No-Cramming" Zone**
Do not open your grammar book less than 12 hours before the exam.

* **Why?** Cramming increases Cortisol (stress hormone), which blocks access to long-term memory.
* **Do this instead:** Watch a German movie with German subtitles. Listen to your favorite German music. Keep the language in your ears, but passively.

**Rule #2: The "Go-Bag" Checklist**
Pack this the night before.

* [ ] **ID Card / Passport (Ausweis/Reisepass):** Without this, you cannot take the exam.
* [ ] **Watch (Analog):** Smartwatches are forbidden. Wall clocks might be broken or far away.
* [ ] **Water & Glucose:** Brains need sugar. Bring a banana or chocolate for the break.
* [ ] **Soft Pencil (HB) & Eraser:** For the answer sheet.

Things **will** go wrong. It is normal. The difference between passing and failing is how you react.

### 🧠 Exercise 1: The "Blackout" Drill (Writing)

**Scenario:** You sit in the Writing Exam. You read the topic. Your mind goes blank. You panic.
**Reaction:** Do not try to write the perfect sentence. Write the **Skeleton**.

**Task:** Write the Skeleton for a formal letter (Complaint) in **60 seconds**.

* Header?
* Greeting?
* First sentence structure?
* Closing?

## 🏃 Part 3: The Final "Brain Activation" Warm-Up

On the morning of the exam, your brain needs to switch from English to German *before* you enter the room. Do these 3 mini-drills while you drink your coffee.

### Drill 1: The "Verb Position" Wake-Up

Fix the word order quickly.

1. **"Ich kann nicht kommen, weil ich bin krank."**
* *Fix:* ...weil ich krank **bin**.


2. **"Gestern ich habe Fußball gespielt."**
* *Fix:* Gestern **habe ich** Fußball gespielt.


3. **"Ich freue mich auf den Urlaub, obwohl ich habe kein Geld."**
* *Fix:* ...obwohl ich kein Geld **habe**.



### Drill 2: The "Preposition" Reflex

Don't think, just answer.

* Wait **for** the bus? -> Warten **auf den** Bus.
* Dream **of** the holiday? -> Träumen **vom (von dem)** Urlaub.
* Interested **in** sports? -> Interessiert **an** Sport.
* Satisfied **with** the car? -> Zufrieden **mit dem** Auto.

### Drill 3: The "Tense" Check

Haben or Sein?

* **Gehen:** Ich *bin* gegangen.
* **Bleiben:** Ich *bin* geblieben.
* **Arbeiten:** Ich *habe* gearbeitet.
* **Passieren:** Es *ist* passiert.
