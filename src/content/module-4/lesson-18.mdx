# 🏛️ Lesson 18: The Speaking Simulation I (Parts 1 & 2)

**Focus:** *Mündliche Prüfung Teil 1 & 2*.
**Goal:** Perfect your introduction and master the art of storytelling without getting stuck.

In the exam, Part 1 is a warm-up, but **Part 2 (Experience)** is where you earn the real points for vocabulary and grammar range.

---

## 🗣️ Part 1: Social Contact (The Warm-Up)

**Time:** 2-3 minutes.
**Task:** Introduce yourself and react to your partner.

### ⚔️ Exercise 1: The "Fossil Error" Check

Many students say these sentences wrong. Fix them.

**1. "I have 25 years."**

* *German Mistake:* Ich habe 25 Jahre.
* *Correction:* Ich **bin** 25 Jahre **alt**.

**2. "I live here for 2 years."**

* *German Mistake:* Ich wohne hier für 2 Jahre.
* *Correction:* Ich wohne hier **seit** 2 Jahren.

**3. "I am engineer for profession."**

* *German Mistake:* Ich bin Ingenieur für Beruf.
* *Correction:* Ich bin **von** Beruf Ingenieur. (Or: Ich arbeite **als** Ingenieur).

## 🗣️ Part 2: The Experience (The Story)

**Time:** 3-4 minutes per person.

**Task:** You get a topic (e.g., "A Trip", "A Concert", "A Sporting Event"). You must tell a story.

Arrange the connectors in the logical order of a story.

### ⚔️ Exercise 4: Building the "Universal Story"

We will build a story about **"A Weekend Trip"**. This fits almost any topic (Travel, Family, Weekend, Hobby).

**Step 1: Introduction (Wann & Wo)**

</InlineBlanks>
**Step 2: The Activity (Was)**

<InlineBlanks>

**Step 4: The Conclusion/Feeling (Wie war es?)**
<InlineBlanks>

</InlineBlanks>

### ⚔️ Exercise 5: Vocabulary Upgrade (A2 -> B1)

Examiners love emotional adjectives. Upgrade the boring sentence.

**1. "Das Essen war gut."**

* *Upgrade:* Das Essen war **lecker** / **köstlich** / **ausgezeichnet**.

**2. "Der Film war interessant."**

* *Upgrade:* Der Film war **spannend** / **bewegend** / **lustig**.

**3. "Ich war froh."**

* *Upgrade:* Ich war **glücklich** / **begeistert** / **zufrieden**.

## 🗣️ Part 2: The Q&A Defense

After you tell your story, your partner (and the examiner) will ask you a question.

### ⚔️ Exercise 6: Answering the "Why" Question

**Partner asks:** *"Warum seid ihr nach München gefahren und nicht nach Berlin?"*

**Strategy:** Use *weil* or *da*.
**Task:** Build the answer.

* *Keywords:* München / näher / sein.
* *Answer:* "Wir sind nach München gefahren, **weil** es näher **ist**."

### ⚔️ Exercise 7: Answering the "Would you recommend it?" Question

**Partner asks:** *"Würdest du das Museum empfehlen?"*

**Strategy:** Use *Auf jeden Fall* (Definitely) or *Eigentlich nicht* (Actually not).
**Task:** Build a positive answer.

* *Answer:* "**Auf jeden Fall!** Das Museum ist sehr modern und man lernt viel."
