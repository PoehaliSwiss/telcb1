# 🏛️ Lesson 1: The Golden Rule – Hauptsatz vs. Nebensatz

**Topic:** *Satzbau* (Word Order) & *Konjunktionen* (Connectors).
**Exam Relevance:**

* **Schreiben (Writing):** If you cannot write a correct "weil" sentence, you will not pass B1 Writing.
* **Sprachbausteine:** This is the #1 tested grammar point in the gap-fill section.
* **Sprechen:** Speaking in short, choppy sentences (A2 style) limits your score. You need flow.

---

## 1. 📘 Deep Theory: Position 0, 1, 2, and End

In German, the verb is the king. Its position determines everything.

### A. The "Hauptsatz" (Main Clause)

The conjugated verb is **always** at **Position 2**.

* *Standard:* Ich **kaufe** heute ein Auto.
* *Inversion:* Heute **kaufe** ich ein Auto. (Position 1 is occupied by "Heute", Verb stays at 2).

**Position 0 Connectors (ADUSO):**
These words do not count. The verb follows the subject.

* **A**ber (but)
* **D**enn (because)
* **U**nd (and)
* **S**ondern (but rather)
* **O**der (or)
* *Example:* Ich bin müde, **aber** ich **gehe** ins Kino.

### B. The "Nebensatz" (Subordinate Clause)

These connectors **kick the verb to the very end**.

* **Weil** (because)
* **Dass** (that)
* **Wenn** (if / when)
* **Obwohl** (although)
* *Example:* Ich gehe ins Kino, **weil** ich den Film **sehen will**.

---

## 2. ⚠️ The "Denn" vs. "Weil" Trap

Both mean "because". You must master switching between them.

* **Denn:** Simple. Like English. (*...denn ich **habe** Hunger.*)
* **Weil:** Advanced. Verb at end. (*...weil ich Hunger **habe**.*)

**Exam Tip:** In the **Writing** part, try to use *weil*. It shows higher grammar control. In **Speaking**, *denn* is a safe "lifesaver" if you get confused with word order.

### Drill 2: Sentence Combination

Combine the sentences. Pay attention to the verb!

**1. Ich lerne Deutsch. Es macht Spaß. (weil)**

* *Solution:* Ich lerne Deutsch, **weil** es Spaß **macht**.

**2. Wir gehen spazieren. Es regnet. (obwohl)**

* *Solution:* Wir gehen spazieren, **obwohl** es **regnet**.

**3. Ich weiß. Du kommst morgen. (dass)**

* *Solution:* Ich weiß, **dass** du morgen **kommst**.

## 5. ✍️ Writing Prep: Expanding Sentences

In the B1 Writing Exam (Letter), simple sentences get you a pass. Complex sentences get you a "Good".

**Task:** Connect the simple sentences to make a B1 sentence.

* *Level A2:* Ich komme nicht. Ich bin krank.
* *Level B1:* Ich kann leider nicht kommen, **weil** ich krank bin.
* *Level A2:* Das Wetter ist schön. Wir bleiben zu Hause.
* *Level B1:* **Obwohl** das Wetter schön ist, bleiben wir zu Hause.

---

## 6. 🗣️ Speaking Strategy: The "Dass" Pause

In **Speaking Part 2**, you have to give your opinion.
Start sentences with:

* *Ich denke, **dass**...* (I think that...)
* *Ich glaube, **dass**...* (I believe that...)
* *Ich finde gut, **dass**...* (I find it good that...)

**Trick:** If you start with "Ich denke, dass...", you immediately buy yourself 2-3 seconds of thinking time while you move the verb to the end mentally.
