# 🏛️ Lesson 2: The Storyteller – Past Tenses (Perfekt vs. Präteritum)

**Topic:** *Vergangenheit* (Talking about the past).
**Exam Relevance:**

* **Schreiben (Writing):** You will almost certainly write a letter/email about something that happened (a party, an accident, a trip).
* **Sprechen (Speaking):** Part 1 (Introduction) involves talking about your past career/education. Part 2 involves narrating an experience.
* **Grammar:** Distinguishing between *haben* and *sein* is a pass/fail skill.

---

## 1. 📘 Deep Theory: The "Two-Past" System

In English, you have Simple Past, Present Perfect, Past Progressive, etc.
In German B1, it is much simpler. We divide the past based on **Usage**, not Time.

### A. Das Perfekt (The Spoken Past)

Used for **Speaking** and **Emails/Letters** (informal/semi-formal).

* **Formula:** `Haben/Sein` (Position 2) + ... + `Partizip II` (End).
* *Example:* Ich **habe** gestern Fußball **gespielt**.

### B. Das Präteritum (The Written Past)

Used in books and newspapers. BUT, at B1, you use it in speech **ONLY** for these specific verbs:

1. **Sein** (war)
2. **Haben** (hatte)
3. **Modal Verbs** (konnte, musste, wollte, durfte)

**The B1 Rule:** Don't say "Ich habe Hunger gehabt." Say "**Ich hatte Hunger.**" It sounds much better. For everything else (kaufen, gehen, machen), use Perfekt.

---

## 2. ⚠️ The Algorithm: Haben or Sein?

This is the most common mistake. Use this flowchart.

1. **Is there Movement from A to B?** (gehen, fahren, fliegen, kommen) -> **SEIN**
2. **Is there a Change of State?** (aufwachen, sterben, einschlafen, wachsen) -> **SEIN**
3. **Are they the exceptions?** (sein, bleiben, passieren, begegnen) -> **SEIN**
4. **Everything else?** -> **HABEN**

### Drill 2: Präteritum for "The Big Three"

Transform the Perfekt into Präteritum (Better Style for B1).

**1. Ich bin krank gewesen.**

* *Solution:* Ich **war** krank.

**2. Ich habe keine Zeit gehabt.**

* *Solution:* Ich **hatte** keine Zeit.

**3. Wir haben arbeiten müssen.**

* *Solution:* Wir **mussten** arbeiten.

## 5. ✍️ Writing Prep: The "Weekend Story"

In the exam, you often have to describe an event.
**Structure:**

1. **Start:** *Letztes Wochenende...* (Position 1).
2. **Verb 1:** *...bin ich...* (Position 2).
3. **Details:** *...mit Freunden nach München...* (Middle).
4. **Verb 2:** *...gefahren.* (End).

**Task:** Reorder the sentence.

<Ordering items={["Letzte","Woche","habe","ich","einen","neuen","Computer","gekauft."]} direction="horizontal" mode="compact" />

**Task:** Construct a "Weil" sentence in the past.

* *Parts:* Ich bin müde. Ich habe viel gearbeitet.
* *Solution:* Ich bin müde, **weil** ich viel gearbeitet **habe**. (Auxiliary moves to the very end!).

---

## 6. 🗣️ Speaking Strategy: Using "War" as an Anchor

In **Speaking Part 1**, examiners ask: "What did you do in your home country?"
Use *war* and *habe* to structure your biography simply.

* *Bad:* "Ich habe Schule besucht. Dann habe ich Studium gemacht." (Grammatically weak).
* *Good:* "Ich **war** Schüler in Spanien. Danach **habe** ich Medizin **studiert**. Später **war** ich als Arzt tätig."
